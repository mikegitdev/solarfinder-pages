SolarFinder

Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026

The short version: SolarFinder collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Your location, camera image and saved places are processed on your device only. The one thing the app sends is an anonymous crash report when something goes wrong, which you can turn off in Settings.

Crash reports

When the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a report to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) so the problem can be fixed. A report contains the error and where in the code it happened, the app version, your device model and system version, and a random installation ID that is not tied to you. It never contains your location, saved places or camera images. Turn Send crash reports off in Settings to stop sending them.

Location

Your current position is used to show the sun path for where you are. It is never transmitted, stored anywhere but your device, or shared. Locations you save stay in your device's local storage. You can refuse or revoke the permission at any time in your system settings; the app also works with locations you pick on the map.

Camera

The AR view opens the camera to overlay sun paths on your surroundings. The image is displayed live and never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Third-party services

The app loads:

  • map tiles from MapTiler AG (© OpenStreetMap contributors),
  • place lookups through the Nominatim service of the OpenStreetMap Foundation, and
  • the app stores' listing data to check for updates.

These providers see your request and IP address as with any website visit; no account, identifier or location history is attached to it. This website itself is served by GitHub Pages; visiting it is an ordinary web request.

What we don't collect

  • No accounts, names, emails or identifiers tied to you
  • No analytics, screen tracking or advertising
  • No location history, camera images or saved places
  • No data sold or shared with anyone

Children

The app is not directed at children, and because it collects no personal data, no personal data of children is collected either.

Your control

Everything the app keeps — saved locations, settings, reference azimuths — lives on your device and is removed completely when you uninstall the app.

Contact and changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears here and in the app. Questions: pythondev40@gmail.com.
